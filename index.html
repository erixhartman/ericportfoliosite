Highlights

Four decisions that changed what the team shipped.

Launching trust capabilities for a new digital bank

Haventree Bank, 2025 to now

Challenge

A new bank needed compliant, scalable foundations for fraud, AML, customer authentication, documentation, internal servicing and performance measurement, before and after launch.

My decision

Under launch pressure, I backed a reusable document framework. We could automate each new document type on the same compliant foundation while the team focused on the customer journeys needed for opening day.

Result

The compliant framework automated delivery of about 50 document types. The bank went on to serve thousands of customers and hold tens of millions in deposits.

Building a new monetization engine for live sports

Sportsnet+, 2022 to 2025

Challenge

Sportsnet+ needed a scalable way to make money from premium live audiences beyond subscriptions, without hurting the viewing experience.

My decision

I worked with sales, ad tech and video engineering to weigh what we could sell against what the player could reliably deliver during a live game. Playback reliability shaped what we offered advertisers.

Result

A new revenue channel generating more than $10M a year.

Turning streaming reliability into a growth lever

Sportsnet+, 2022 to 2025

Challenge

Playback friction was driving user exits, support demand, dissatisfaction and churn.

My decision

I moved playback performance up the roadmap for six months. We paired viewing behaviour with player telemetry and engineering diagnostics to prioritize the failures that were costing fans the game.

Result

Streaming quality metrics up 48%, NPS up 10%, churn down 21%.

Scaling identity across a national retail ecosystem

Loblaw Digital, PCid, 2020 to 2022

Challenge

One identity platform had to serve more than a million daily users while balancing security, conversion and the needs of several Loblaw businesses.

My decision

I challenged a proposal to require two-factor authentication for everyone. We tested the sign-in friction and took the measured conversion impact back to the businesses before committing to the rollout.

Result

Single sign-on across PCid businesses, and experiments that helped avoid an estimated $2M in revenue loss during a security integration.

What it does

Donna Walks

React, TypeScript, Vite, Vercel Open the siteCode on GitHub

What it does

A walk log for Donna, my reactive Lab: distance, bathroom breaks and every reaction, with what set her off, how close it was and how strong.

Design decision

A walk log has to work with a leash in one hand. GPS handles distance and the phone stores the entries. That keeps our routes private, with the tradeoff that the history lives on one device.

How I operate

What it's like to work with me.

I want to be challenged, and I'll challenge back.

Tell me where my thinking has a gap. I'd rather hear it before the decision than after the launch. If something's off, I'll raise it early, kindly and directly.

I assume positive intent, especially when we disagree.

The people I work with are trying to get it right too. I'll push hard on an idea and still treat the person behind it with respect.

I separate what I know from what I'm guessing.

I'll tell you which numbers are measured and which are estimates, so nobody builds a plan on a guess. When a decision is expensive to undo, I test first.

I ship small and own the outcome.

I'd rather put something small and live in front of users than polish a prototype, and I fix what users feel before the wishlist. Shipping is the start: I'm on the hook for whether it moved the number. At Sportsnet, a six-month stability project cut churn by 21%.

I get calmer when the pressure goes up.

Launch days and incidents need someone steady. I keep the team focused on the next decision.
