Personal toolAndroid web app

Donna Walks

A walk log for my leash-reactive Lab. One tap logs a reaction, a pee or a poo, with GPS distance and a trend of reactions per kilometre.

Problem

Our trainer and vet kept asking how often she reacts, how close, and whether it's improving. I was answering from memory.

Key decision

No backend. Data stays on my phone so logging works on trails with no signal. Reactions per km is the headline metric, so long walks don't look like bad weeks.

That's ball, baby!

MLB leaderboards rebuilt every morning: the top 50 hitters and pitchers, plus which big contracts are paying off.

Problem

A stats site I built with a friend had gone unmaintained, and I wanted the leaderboards back in a cleaner form.

Key decision

I calculate wRC+, FIP and contract value myself from MLB's free data instead of scraping sites whose terms don't allow it. A scheduled job rebuilds it all daily.
