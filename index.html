Turning streaming reliability into a growth lever

Sportsnet+, 2022 to 2025

Challenge

Approach

A focused six-month program using quality telemetry, customer behaviour, engineering diagnostics and prioritized fixes across the viewing experience.

How I operate

What it's like to work with me.

I want to be challenged.

Tell me where my thinking has a gap. I'd rather hear it before the decision than after the launch.

I assume positive intent, especially when we disagree.

The people I work with are trying to get it right too. I'll push hard on an idea and still treat the person behind it with respect.

I separate what I know from what I'm guessing.

I'll tell you which numbers are measured and which are estimates, so nobody builds a plan on a guess.

I started as an engineer, and it shows.

I can follow a technical tradeoff, and I know how to discuss estimates with developers.

I test before I bet.

When a decision is expensive to undo, I'd rather run the experiment first.

I ship small and learn fast.

I'd rather put something small and live in front of users than polish a prototype. A week of real use teaches more than a month of guessing.

I fix what users feel first.

The wishlist can wait while people are hitting problems. At Sportsnet, a six-month stability project cut churn by 21%.

I own the outcome.

Shipping the spec is the start. I'm on the hook for whether it actually moved the number.

I watch what people do, not just what they say.

I studied psychology before I wrote code. I start with how users and teammates actually behave, then design for that.

I build things to understand them.

My side projects are things I actually use. Building them is how I learned what it takes to ship with AI tools.

I get calmer when the pressure goes up.

Launch days and incidents need someone steady. I keep the team focused on the next decision.

I'll have the hard conversation.

If something's off, I'd rather raise it now, kindly and directly, than let it get bigger.

I help people grow.

Some of my favourite work has been helping someone land their next role.
